# Research: A real not-found page

## D1 - Search on the page as well as in the header

**Decision**: A search form on the page, sending to the catalogue the way the header does.

**Rationale**: On a phone the header's search is behind the menu; the person on this page was looking for something, and the box in front of them is the shortest way on.

**Alternatives rejected**: Suggesting products - a guess at what they wanted, and a request on a page that should never fail.
